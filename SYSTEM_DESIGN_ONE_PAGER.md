# AI Workforce Simulation — One Pager

## 1. What is this?

A simulation game where users manage AI employees to complete real-world tasks and compete for profit.

Users do NOT prompt AI directly.

They:
→ hire AI workers  
→ assign tasks  
→ optimize workflows  
→ compete economically  

---

## 2. Core Concept

This is NOT an AI tool.

This is:
→ a multi-agent system  
→ a management game  
→ a simulated economy  

---

## 3. Gameplay Loop

Manage → Execute → Evaluate → Profit → Optimize

---

## 4. Key Mechanics

### 4.1 AI Employees (Agents)

- Each has:
  - personality
  - skills
  - flaws

- They are:
  ❌ not perfect  
  ✅ intentionally imperfect  

---

### 4.2 Workflow System

Users design:

- multi-step pipelines
- team compositions

Example:

Research → Write → Edit

---

### 4.3 Evaluation System

Every output is:

- scored
- compared
- converted into profit

---

### 4.4 Asset System

Outputs become assets:

- content
- strategies
- plans

Assets can:

- be reused
- be traded (future)

---

### 4.5 Industry System (RPG Layer)

Users choose ONE role:

- Content
- Marketing
- Product
- Business

Each role:

- has strengths
- has limitations

---

### 4.6 Market System (Future)

Industries interact via:

→ buying and selling outputs  

---

## 5. Why It’s Different

Unlike AI tools:

- users don’t just “get answers”
- they **manage systems**

Unlike games:

- tasks are real-world relevant
- skills transfer to real life

---

## 6. Core Insight

AI makes execution cheap.

The real skill becomes:

→ managing imperfect agents  
→ designing effective workflows  
→ optimizing systems  

---

## 7. Target Users

- creators
- indie hackers
- students
- knowledge workers

---

## 8. MVP Scope

- single industry (Content)
- 3 agents
- 5 tasks
- scoring + profit

---

## 9. Vision

A new interaction paradigm:

→ from “using AI”  
→ to “managing AI organizations”
